How Comet Center works

Idea to part in five moves

Every student follows the same path, whether it's your first Benchy or a 24-hour print on the Giga.

Step 01 · Triage

What are you making?

Four questions. You never need to know machine names. Describe the object and we'll do the translation.

The rules, in three colours

Access levels

Every machine at Comet Center wears a coloured band, the same one printed on its poster. Your passes have to match before the machine is yours.

Self run

Watch, quiz, done. Run it alone whenever the lab is open. Failures cost plastic, not fingers.

Induction first

Pass the module, then a staff member signs you off on the machine. No solo runs until then.

Staff run only

You learn to prepare the job. Staff set up and start it. Submit your file at the front desk.

Step 02 · Machine bay

Every machine at Comet Center

Drag any model to spin it. Open a machine for its station card, the rules, and its training module.

Cost estimator

What will my part cost?

Pick a machine and drop in your file. 3D jobs take STL or OBJ. Flat cutting jobs (waterjet, router, wire EDM) take a DXF outline. You get the price, the job time and the material straight away.
