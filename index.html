How Comet Center works

Idea to part in five moves

Every student follows the same path, whether it's your first Benchy or a 24-hour print on the Giga.

Step 01 · Triage

What are you making?

Four questions. You never need to know machine names. Describe the object and we'll do the translation.

The rules, in three colours

Access levels

Every machine at Comet Center wears a coloured band, the same one printed on its poster. It tells you how much help you need before you run it.

Self run

Read the SOP, watch the videos, then run it alone whenever the lab is open. Failures cost plastic, not fingers.

Induction first

Watch the videos, then a staff member inducts you on the machine. No solo runs until then.

Staff run only

You learn to prepare the job. Staff set up and start it. Submit your file at the front desk.

Step 02 · Machine bay

Every machine at Comet Center

Drag any model to spin it. Open a machine for its station card, the rules, its videos and its SOP.

Self run you use it on your own · Induction first staff show you once, then it's yours · Staff run only staff operate it for you.

Cost estimator

What will my part cost?

Pick a machine and drop in your file. 3D jobs take STL or OBJ. Flat cutting jobs (waterjet, router, wire EDM) take a DXF outline. You get the price, the job time and the material straight away.

Generate your job

We'll create a job sheet (PDF) with your estimate and a payment QR. Bring it to the Comet Center desk (9–10 AM). Staff confirm the price, you pay in front of them, and your job joins the queue.

Your file is uploaded with the job, so staff get it directly. A Google Drive link is only needed for files over the upload limit.

Sign in
